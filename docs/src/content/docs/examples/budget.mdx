## What to notice

- `records.labels` fixes the records: none can be added or removed, and the labels can't be changed.
- The Travel cap is a table rule in code; it pins each issue to its record and month, so the right cells are marked.
- The total is a single cell with `formula: 'require'` and `readOnly: true`.
- The workbook's `validate` is async. It runs after edits, and its result is replaced as soon as the workbook changes again.
- `saveWithErrors: 'confirm'` lets people save with errors after confirming.
