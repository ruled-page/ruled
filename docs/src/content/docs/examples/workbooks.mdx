Without a schema, the editor is a spreadsheet. Switch between the sample workbooks below.

<LiveEditor example="workbooks" height={520}>
  Try: change a number and watch the formulas and charts that use it; right-click a pivot table and choose <strong>Show Field List</strong>; right-click a
  range and insert a chart; drag a chart by its frame.
</LiveEditor>

- **Kitchen sink**: formatting, conditional formats, validation, notes, tables, names, dynamic arrays, `LET` and `LAMBDA`, and checkboxes.
- **Charts**: radar, bubble, stock and 3-D charts, drawn from the cells.
- **Pivot table**: units by region from a hundred orders, worked out again as they change.
- **Orders**: a list to sort, filter and fill.

See [Excel fidelity](/guides/excel-fidelity/) for what the editor shows, edits and keeps.
